Verification scripts and data for

  "Explicit Finite-Order Lower Bounds for High-Order Robust Composite Z Rotations"

Contents
--------
verify_num.py            independent re-verification of the numerical sequences of
                         Section 8 and Appendix G: rebuilds every propagator with
                         scipy.linalg.expm, recomputes the Taylor coefficients of the
                         propagator twice (truncated convolution in double precision and
                         an exact matrix series at 50 decimal digits, mpmath), evaluates
                         the deviation delta(eps) and its logarithmic slope, and writes
                         num_results.json and num_tables.tex (the LaTeX rows of Table 2,
                         so that the table of the paper cannot drift away from the data).

make_outputs.py          regenerates Figure 1 (fignum_num.pdf), the coherent-versus-
                         incoherent balance of Figure 2 (figphys.pdf), and Appendix G
                         (appendix_seqs_num.tex) from num_results.json, and checks the
                         round trip: the angles printed in Appendix G are rounded to 12
                         decimals of pi, and feeding them back reproduces the accuracy
                         statements of Table 2 (nominal mismatch below 9e-10, flatness
                         residual below 1.5e-10).  The parameters of Figure 2 are those
                         used in Section 12 of the paper (GAMMA, EPSTRY at the top of the
                         physics() function).

num_results.json         the verified data: per order N, the angles, the cost, the nominal
                         mismatch, the flatness residual rho_N = max_k ||u_k|| / tau^k,
                         the deviation delta(eps) on a grid, and the local slopes.

num_tables.tex           the LaTeX rows of Table 2, generated by verify_num.py.
                         (Table 1 is generated by hand in main.tex.)

solutions_largeN.json    the raw output of the optimiser that produced the angles (input
                         of verify_num.py).

Requirements: Python 3 with numpy, scipy, mpmath and matplotlib (the figures) --
tested with numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, mpmath 1.3.0, matplotlib 3.11.1.

Run, in this order:

    python3 verify_num.py      # rewrites num_results.json and num_tables.tex
    python3 make_outputs.py    # rewrites fignum_num.pdf and appendix_seqs_num.tex

All matrix norms are operator norms, as in the paper. verify_num.py needs
solutions_largeN.json in the working directory.

Web front end: https://show.dytchem.cn/bloch/
